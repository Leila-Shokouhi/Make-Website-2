Switzerland

Straddling the border between Switzerland and Italy, the Matterhorn is the most distinctive of all the alpine peaks in Europe, jutting up alone about the skyline like a shark’s tooth with 4,478 meters height. Lording over the quaint and charming town of Zermatt, deep in the heart of the Swiss Alps, it’s just about as picturesque as you can imagine.

Australia

There are a handful of pink lakes dotted around Western Australia. There used to be even more, but they can change color. Lake Spencer/Pink Lake sits right by the town of Esperance, which used to be hot pink but slowly changed to blue in 2017. The general consensus is that the construction of the nearby South Coast Highway altered the water flow into the lake, reducing its salinity.

USA Hawaii

The Stairway To Heaven hike in Hawaii is epic, scary, interesting and slightly illegal. This trail in Oahu takes you to the top of the Ko’olau mountains via 3,922 metal stairs of pure adrenaline and doom. At some points, the stairs of death are almost vertical, clinging to the side of the steep mountain. Still, thousands of people make the climb every year, and for good reason! Not only are the views out of this world, but the whole experience is just beyond awesome and unique.

Egypt

What kind of bucket list doesn’t include the Great Pyramids of Giza in Egypt? Believe it or not, the Giza pyramids are fairly easy to visit on a day trip from Cairo, the capital of Egypt. Built in 2560 BC, the Great Pyramid of Giza was the tallest man-made object in the world for 3,800 years. It’s the oldest of the Seven Wonders Of The Ancient World, and the only one still standing today.

India

The Taj Mahal is a huge monument, palace, and tomb built in 1632 by emperor Shah Jahan for his favorite wife, Mumtaz Mahal. The couple is buried together inside the Taj Mahal. The white marble dome was built by 20,000 artisans and the project took decades to finish. It’s surrounded by a 40 acre garden with pools, fountains, and decorative walls. It gets over 8 million visitors per year.

Japan

The Kinkaku-ji Temple (also known as the Golden Pavilion) is one of the most iconic, mesmerizing and famous places in Japan. A good time to visit is in the morning before the wind picks up, then you can see reflections on the pond.

Indonesia

If you want to see orangutans in Borneo, one of the best places to do it is at the Tanjung Puting National Park in Kalimantan, Indonesia. Tanjung Puting is normally accessed by boat via the Sekonyer river. But the river moves very slowly, so you don’t have to worry about seasickness.

South Africa

In South Africa, the incredible Jabulani Safaris is a great supporter of elephants and their conservation for over 20 years. You can observe the elephant as they swim and forage and even walk with the herd at sunset as they are taken to their stables for the night. There will be many excellent opportunities for photo ops during your stay here!

Arctic Canada

Is this place for real? At over 70° N, Coningham Bay is not easy to access. But there is a place in there where polar bears hunt beluga whales. That sounds like some Planet Earth documentary, rare and uncommon. It's not something people can usually see.
