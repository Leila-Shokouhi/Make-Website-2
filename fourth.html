About me:

I'm Leila, the girl behind this website. I'm an American who's been traveling the world full time for 9 years now. I started this travel blog in 2025 to document my own international trips, share my photos, and help others learn how to travel the world and find some really good spots off the beaten path. All of the writing and pictures on this site come from my own personal experience.

I hope my travel blog can help and inspire you in your own journeys as well!
